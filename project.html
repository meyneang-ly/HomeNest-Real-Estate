OUR PROJECT
THE HOMENEST

The Homenest is the first project developed by Homenest Real Estate nestled along National Road 5. A 300-hectare site in Prek Phnov District in Phnom Penh is undergoing a dramatic metamorphosis that will elevate and enrich people's lives. In the greenest neighborhood of Phnom Penh, nature combines seamlessly with cutting-edge contemporary architecture to create The HomeNest, a sophisticated, exciting, and vibrant new mixed-use destination.

The HomeNest combines highly innovative ideas from national and international experts, as well as adding a features mixed infrastructure with luxury residential homes with a beautiful large garden, in its special purpose of creating sophisticated community living in a multi-faceted development project where family and friends can gather to enjoy rich daily living with feature mixed infrastructure including a central park, garden, fitness clubhouse, wedding hall, and many more luxury facilities along the Prek Taten's Canal for the latest lifestyles.
